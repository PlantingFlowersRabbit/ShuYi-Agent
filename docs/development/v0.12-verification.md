## Performance Rationale

The v0.11 slow path rendered the whole uploaded novel into a controlled UI field and eagerly loaded first-chapter
paragraphs. v0.12 keeps the full text in a ref, renders only a small preview, indexes chapter offsets first, and extracts
chapter bodies lazily when selected.
